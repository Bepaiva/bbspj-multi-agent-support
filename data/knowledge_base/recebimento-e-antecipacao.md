# When do I receive my sales? (prazos de recebimento)

Settlement time depends on the payment method and on the receiving plan in the merchant's contract:

- **Debit (débito):** paid in up to 1 business day (D+1) on most plans.
- **Credit in one payment (crédito à vista):** standard offers pay in 2 business days (D+2) into Get Conta. Some plans pay credit at D+30.
- **"Receba Já" plan:** credit and installment sales are received in 2 days (D+2) or 30 days (D+30), as chosen. With D+2 there is a single fee for debit and credit à vista.
- **Installments financed by the merchant (parcelado lojista):** the first installment is paid at D+31 and each remaining installment every 30 days - unless the merchant advances them (see antecipação).
- **Installments financed by the issuer (parcelado emissor):** the merchant receives the full amount at once, in 2 to 31 days depending on the plan.
- **Crediário:** the full amount is paid in one payment within 2 business days of capture, regardless of the number of installments.
- **Prepaid cards:** debit in up to 1 business day; credit in up to 2 business days.
- **Get Conta:** sales fall into the account in the morning, within 2 business days.
- **Payment link (e-commerce):** after 6 months of use, the merchant can request D+2 receiving, subject to analysis.

"Business days" (dias úteis) exclude weekends and national holidays, so a sale made on a Friday with D+1 is paid on Monday.

Merchants can check their payment calendar (agenda de recebíveis / lançamentos futuros) in the Getnet Brasil app or in the Minha Conta portal.

# Receivables advance - antecipação de vendas / antecipação de recebíveis

## What it is
Antecipação lets the merchant receive the value of **credit card sales** (à vista or parcelado) before the scheduled settlement date, paying an advance fee (taxa de antecipação). Debit sales cannot be advanced because they are already paid the next day.

## How it works
- A sale can be advanced from D+1 (one business day after the sale) until D-3 (three business days before its original settlement date). Example: a sale on 04/03 with settlement on 03/04 can be advanced from 05/03 up to 31/03.
- Only sales captured by Getnet can be advanced through Getnet; brands enabled with another acquirer must be advanced with that acquirer.
- The cost is the advance fee, which is proportional to how many days earlier the money is received, on top of the normal MDR and rent. Rates are defined per contract and shown in the app before confirming.

## Types of advance
- **Eventual (one-off) advance:** the merchant chooses which sales/amounts to advance when needed - through the Getnet Brasil app, the Minha Conta portal, the POS terminal or the call centre.
- **Automatic advance (antecipação automática):** all eligible credit sales are advanced automatically every day. For clients on the "Receba conforme suas vendas" plan, automatic advance is a condition for certain fee exemptions; Getnet evaluates each contract individually.
- **Santander account holders** can also request it with their account manager or via Santander Internet Banking (business accounts, when enabled), Monday to Friday 9am-9pm.

## Where to check
Future entries: Consultas > Lançamentos Futuros. Advanced sales appear the day after the advance under Consultas > Extratos por período > Antecipação por período.

## Gravame, trava and cessão
- **Trava de domicílio bancário** (old mechanism) locked all receivables of a brand to a bank as loan collateral.
- **Gravame** replaced it: only the pledged share of receivables goes to the bank's account; the rest can go to an account the merchant chooses.
- **Cessão** transfers ownership of receivables to a third party.
- Since 7 June 2021 (Resolution 4734 / Circular 3952) the merchant must authorise Getnet to consult their receivables schedule at the registrar.
