# Getnet Crediário

Crediário lets the merchant's customer split a purchase into **up to 48 installments (48x)** on their credit card.

## How many installments?
Up to 48x. The **maximum term and the interest rate are defined by the card issuer** (the customer's bank), so the number of installments actually available varies from card to card. The terminal simulation shows the installment value and total cost before the sale.

## Who grants the credit and who pays interest?
- The card issuer grants the credit and approves it at contracting time (the simulation is only a consultation, subject to credit analysis).
- **Interest is paid by the cardholder (the buyer), not the merchant.**
- The merchant pays only the discount rate (MDR) defined in the Getnet contract.

## When does the merchant receive?
The **full amount in one payment, within 2 business days** after capture, regardless of the number of installments, with the MDR deducted. There is no advance (antecipação) cost.

## Enabling Crediário
- Getnet must approve the merchant; request it through the Getnet Brasil app or the call centre. It can also be disabled on request.
- Check status in the app: Meu negócio > Taxas e modalidades > Cartões, or in the Portal do Cliente.
- If it is enabled but does not appear on the terminal: run an acquiring transaction (Mastercard, Visa, Elo or Amex) or initialise the terminal (Suporte > Bandeiras > Funções Técnicas > Inicialização).

## Selling with Crediário on the machine
1. Insert, tap or swipe the card. 2. Select credit. 3. Enter the amount. 4. Choose "Crediário". 5. Enter the number of installments. 6. Wait for processing. 7. Choose the installment plan. 8. On the summary, contract, simulate again, print the simulation or cancel. 9. Customer reviews and enters the PIN. 10. Transaction approved.

Note: Crediário sales do not count toward the revenue threshold for rent waiver (aluguel zero).
