# Getnet Payment Link (Link de Pagamento)

The Payment Link lets merchants sell remotely - **through WhatsApp, Instagram and other social networks, e-mail or SMS** - without a card machine or an online store. The merchant creates a link and sends it; the customer opens it, chooses the payment method and pays.

## Can I sell through WhatsApp?
Yes. Selling on WhatsApp is one of the main use cases: create the link and use the share/copy option to send it in a WhatsApp chat (or any other channel).

## Features
- Payment methods for the buyer: credit card, debit, Pix and boleto (digital wallets are also accepted).
- Customisable link with product image; several products in the same cart.
- Shipping cost can be included in the charge.
- Getnet anti-fraud included.
- Accepts foreign buyers who provide an identification document (RNE).

## How to create a link
1. Open the Minha Conta portal (login and password) or the Getnet Brasil app. Links can also be created via API.
2. Go to "Produtos e Serviços" > "Link de Pagamento" > "Criar Link de Pagamento".
3. Fill in product, shipping and payment options.
4. Tap share/copy and send it through the channel you want (e.g. WhatsApp).

## Plans and costs (reference)
- **Inicial:** no monthly fee; R$ 1,00 per paid link (you only pay when the payment is approved).
- **Básico:** R$ 28/month including 60 card transactions; R$ 0,50 per extra transaction.
- **Avançado:** R$ 60/month including 150 transactions.
- Plus the card fee (MDR), which varies with installments and receiving term. The site simulator shows 1,90% for Mastercard, Visa and Elo (new clients: PF, MEI and PJ with revenue up to R$ 3 million/year; subject to credit analysis).

## Receiving
Payment-link (e-commerce) sales follow their own schedule; after 6 months of use the merchant can request D+2 receiving, subject to analysis, and advances of e-commerce sales are blocked for the first 6 months. Prepaid cards: debit in up to 1 business day, credit up to 2 business days.

## Inactivity
If the merchant goes more than 4 months without transacting, the Payment Link is disabled and must go through approval again.
