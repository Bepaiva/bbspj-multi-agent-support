# Getnet customer service channels (atendimento)

- **Capitals and metropolitan areas:** 4002-4000 or 4003-4000 - every day, 06h to 22h.
- **Other locations:** 0800 648 8000 - every day, 06h to 22h.
- **WhatsApp:** (11) 97694-6815 - every day, 06h to 22h.
- **Hearing/speech impaired:** 0800 771 0301.
- **Ombudsman (Ouvidoria):** 0800 646 3404 - Monday to Friday, 09h15 to 17h30 (except holidays).
- **Online:** Fale Conosco, Central de Ajuda (https://site.getnet.com.br/duvidas/) and the Minha Conta portal (https://minhaconta.getnet.com.br).
- **Getnet Brasil app (SuperGet Mobile)** on Google Play and the App Store: check sales and receivables, advance sales, register Pix keys, request maintenance, create payment links and simulate rates.

# Other Getnet Brazil products
- **Get Checkout / e-commerce:** online payments with anti-fraud.
- **Recurring payments (pagamentos recorrentes):** charge monthly subscriptions.
- **Split:** split sales between parties.
- **TEF solutions:** POS TEF, conciliator, 80+ brands for counter automation.
- **Empréstimo:** loans for merchants. **Recargas:** mobile top-ups with commission.
- **Portal do Cartão BNDES**, **Currency converter** for foreign currency, **Agentic Commerce**.

# About Getnet
Getnet is the payments company of Grupo Santander, operating across Latin America (Brazil, Mexico, Chile, Argentina, Uruguay) and Europe (Spain, Portugal and other markets), serving more than a million merchants. Globally it offers in-person payments, online payments, omnichannel solutions, value-added services, financing and agentic commerce infrastructure that lets businesses accept AI-agent-initiated payments (https://www.getnet.net/en).
