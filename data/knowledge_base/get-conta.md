# Get Conta (Getnet digital account)

Get Conta is Getnet's free digital account, offered in partnership with Dock. It lets merchants receive all their sales (cards and Pix) in one place without needing a traditional bank account.

- Free account with no hidden fees and a free virtual card for purchases.
- Send and receive money, use Pix (register keys in the app under Conta > Pix > Minhas Chaves), transfer, pay bills and withdraw at Banco24Horas ATMs.
- Card sales fall into Get Conta in the morning, within 2 business days.
- Receivables advance (antecipação) is available.
- Who can open: individuals (PF) and companies, including MEI, EI, EIRELI, franchises and LTDA.
- How to open: when ordering a machine on the site, choose to receive in the account; without a machine, use digital registration ("Sem máquina").
- Managed in the Getnet Brasil app (Google Play / App Store).
