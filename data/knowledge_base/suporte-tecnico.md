# Card machine won't connect to the internet (maquininha sem conexão)

Getnet chip machines use a **multi-operator chip (Chip Algar)** that connects to several carriers, working anywhere with 2G/3G (or 4G on Get Smart) coverage. Most models also support Wi-Fi.

Recommended troubleshooting steps:
1. **Restart the machine.** Turn it off and on (the key combination depends on the model - e.g. hold the red key for 5 seconds to turn off and the green key to turn on).
2. **Check signal and Wi-Fi.** Look at the signal/Wi-Fi icon. If using Wi-Fi, confirm the router has internet and re-enter the network/password in the terminal's settings. Move closer to a window or to the router.
3. **Check the chip.** With the machine off, open the back cover and make sure the chip is seated in the SIM1 or SIM2 slot (metal side down). Close the cover, turn on and wait for the 2G/3G/4G network to appear on screen.
4. **Run the communication test:** press the function key (F, # or equivalent) > 60 > option 2 "Manutenção" > option 1 "Teste de comunicação".
5. **Charge the battery** - a low battery can drop the modem. Charge for at least 3 hours.
6. **Initialise the terminal** if transactions still fail after the network is up: Suporte > Bandeiras > Funções Técnicas > Inicialização.
7. If the problem persists, contact support (4002-4000 in capitals/metro areas, 0800 648 8000 elsewhere, 06h-22h, or WhatsApp (11) 97694-6815). Getnet replaces defective machines under its lifetime maintenance while the contract is active.

# Transaction declined (transação negada / recusada)

Most declines come from the **card issuer**, not from the machine. Common reasons: insufficient funds/limit, wrong PIN, expired or blocked card, card not enabled for that function (e.g. credit vs debit), installments not allowed, or suspected fraud. What to do:
- Read the code/message on screen or on the receipt and check it against the decline-code guide (e.g. 51 insufficient funds, 55 wrong PIN, 54 expired card, 57 transaction not permitted for the card, 05 not authorised by issuer).
- Ask the customer to try again, use another function (debit/credit) or another card, or contact their bank.
- If many different cards are declined, the problem is likely the terminal or connectivity: check the connection, run the communication test and initialise the terminal.
- Get Smart duplicate rule: an identical sale (same card, brand and amount) within 5 minutes is cancelled automatically - wait 5 minutes or change the amount.
- Brand not enabled for the merchant (e.g. a voucher not contracted) also causes declines - check enabled brands in the app.

# Cancellations, refunds and reports
- Same-day sales can be refunded on the terminal: Suporte > Estorno, with the merchant password.
- Sales from previous days are cancelled only through the call centre (4003-4000).
- Chargeback happens when a cardholder disputes a transaction with their bank (e.g. goods not delivered, unrecognised transaction, duplicate processing); the amount is debited from the merchant.
- Sales reports (daily summary, partial/final closing, detailed closing) are under the Support menu and require the merchant password.
- A forgotten merchant password must be reset through the call centre.
