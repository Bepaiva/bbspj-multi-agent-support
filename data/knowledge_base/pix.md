# Pix with Getnet

Pix is the Brazilian Central Bank's instant payment system: transfers settle in seconds, 24 hours a day including weekends and holidays. Getnet lets merchants accept Pix in physical stores and online.

## Ways to receive Pix with Getnet
- **QR Code on the card machine** (dynamic QR Code generated by the terminal - Get Mini, Get Clássica and Get Smart).
- **Pix por aproximação** (contactless Pix, tapping the phone on the machine).
- **Pix by biometrics** (new).
- **Get Tap** - the phone works as the card machine.
- **Payment Link and e-commerce (Get Checkout)** - the buyer can choose Pix.
- **TEF** solutions (e.g. Auttar) can also generate Pix QR Codes.

## Do I need a bank account to receive Pix sales?
Yes - Pix sales are credited to the account linked to the Pix key registered with Getnet, so the merchant needs an account that has an active Pix key. There are two options:
1. **Get Conta** - Getnet's free digital account (no bank needed). Pix keys can be registered in the Getnet Brasil app: Conta > Pix > Minhas Chaves > Cadastrar chave. Sales received in Get Conta fall within 2 business days for cards, while Pix is credited quickly.
2. **A bank account (domicílio bancário)** - Getnet's Pix acquiring FAQ lists a Santander bank account with an active Pix key as the standard requirement; availability for other banks (PSPs) depends on integration and should be confirmed with the call centre (4002-4000 / 0800 648 8000).

So: a traditional bank account is not mandatory if the merchant uses Get Conta, but some account with an active Pix key is required.

## How to enable Pix on Getnet
Through the Minha Conta portal or the Getnet Brasil app, informing the Pix key registered at the receiving account (the key must belong to the account where you want to receive). Requirements: a compatible capture device (terminal, TEF or e-commerce), an active Pix key and the registration with the legal representative.

## Timing
Pix is usually credited in seconds, but Getnet warns that depending on the Pix model the merchant qualifies for and the approval time of the sale, the credit can take up to one business day.

## Pix keys - limits and tips
- Key types: CPF/CNPJ, e-mail, mobile number or random key (32 characters).
- Individuals can have up to 5 keys per account; companies up to 20. A key can only be registered at one institution.
- Getnet only needs one key linked to the receiving account.
- Tip: prefer a random key or the CNPJ, because the key data is visible to the payer.
