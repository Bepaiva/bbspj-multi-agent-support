# Getnet card machines (maquininhas)

Getnet (part of Grupo Santander) offers a family of card machines for individuals (CPF), MEIs and companies (CNPJ). The machines are rented (comodato / aluguel), not sold. Every machine accepts debit, credit (including installments - parcelado), contactless/tap (aproximação, NFC), Pix via QR Code and 80+ card brands and vouchers. Getnet advertises lifetime maintenance of the equipment while the contract is active, requested through the Getnet Brasil app (SuperGet).

Sales on the machines are received in the Get Conta digital account in 2 business days (D+2) on the standard offer; debit can be received in 1 business day depending on the plan.

## Get Mini
- Entry-level, compact model - aimed at people starting out and individual sellers (pessoa física).
- Connectivity: Wi-Fi plus a 3G data chip with free data.
- Digital receipts (SMS/e-mail); a "Get Mini com bobina" variant prints paper receipts.
- Colour display.
- Monthly rent shown on the Getnet homepage: R$ 14,90 (offers change; check the site).

## Get Clássica
- The traditional, mid-range POS terminal.
- Prints paper receipts (bobina) and can also send digital receipts.
- Long-lasting battery.
- Connectivity: data chip (multi-operator chip, 2G/3G) and Wi-Fi.
- Accepts chip, magstripe, contactless (NFC) and Pix QR Code.
- Monthly rent: R$ 79,90. Rent can be waived (aluguel zero) when monthly card revenue exceeds the threshold of the current offer (for example R$ 20 mil/month on some offers). Voucher (VA/VR) and Crediário sales do not count toward the waiver threshold.

## Get Smart
- The most complete model: an Android smart POS.
- Connectivity: 4G chip with free data, Wi-Fi fallback and Bluetooth.
- Prints paper receipts and sends digital receipts.
- Supports third-party management apps installed from the Getstore (Get Smart developer portal) - e.g. stock control, invoices (NF-e) and sales management. App subscriptions are billed by each app developer.
- Payments: credit, debit, voucher, installment credit, crediário, split, QR Code (Pix) and NFC.
- Monthly rent: R$ 129,90; can be waived above the revenue threshold of the current offer (e.g. R$ 40 mil or R$ 80 mil/month).
- Duplicate-protection rule: two identical sales (same card, brand and amount) at the same merchant within 5 minutes - the second one is cancelled automatically. To repeat a legitimate sale, wait 5 minutes or use a different amount.
- Sales from previous days can only be cancelled through the call centre (4003-4000).
- Battery tip: charge for at least 3 hours at a time and recharge at around 20% instead of letting it fully discharge.

## Get Clássica vs Get Smart - key differences
| | Get Clássica | Get Smart |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Traditional POS | Android smart POS |
| Mobile data | 2G/3G multi-operator chip | 4G chip |
| Wi-Fi | Yes | Yes (fallback) |
| Bluetooth | No | Yes |
| Third-party apps (Getstore) | No | Yes |
| Printer | Yes | Yes |
| Monthly rent (list) | R$ 79,90 | R$ 129,90 |
| Card fees (MDR) | Same fee table | Same fee table |

Both use the same fee table - the difference is hardware, connectivity and the app ecosystem. Get Smart suits businesses that want management apps on the terminal or operate in areas where 4G/Wi-Fi fallback matters; Get Clássica is the cheaper option for straightforward card acceptance.

## Get Tap - sell with your phone (maquininha no celular)
- Turns an NFC-enabled Android phone (Android 12 or higher) into a card machine - no physical terminal.
- No rent, no adhesion fee and no maintenance fee.
- Accepts debit, credit (up to 12x), Pix and vouchers (subject to eligibility). Brands: Mastercard, Visa, Elo, Amex, Hipercard.
- Fees start at 1,69% (debit) and 3,79% (credit 1x), up to 12,88% (credit 12x) - "a partir de" rates.
- Available to individuals, MEIs and companies with annual revenue up to R$ 3 million (new clients), subject to credit analysis.
- Activated through the Getnet Brasil app.

## Fees (MDR) - reference values
Getnet's public offers show fees starting at 1,69% for debit and 3,79% for 1x credit, reaching about 12,88% for 12x credit, for receiving in Get Conta. Personalized rates are available to companies selling about R$ 5 mil/month or more (simulation at https://site.getnet.com.br/taxas-personalizadas/). Rates change and depend on the plan, so customers should always confirm the current values in the Getnet Brasil app or the Minha Conta portal.

## How to order a machine
Order on site.getnet.com.br (PJ offers at /todas-as-maquininhas, PF offers at /ofertas), choose the model, quantity and carrier (for chip models) and finish the order. Shipping is free across Brazil. After delivery the machine must be activated. Someone who received a machine transferred from another person must register to activate it in their own name.
